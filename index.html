FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is FacelessAI?

FacelessAI is an AI-powered short-form video creation tool. You provide a topic and the system generates a script, voice and video using the connected backend services.

Which platforms can I create videos for?

The interface supports YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels. Videos are designed around the vertical 9:16 format.

Do I need video editing software?

No separate editing software is required for the basic generation workflow. FacelessAI handles the main generation process through its backend.

Does FacelessAI generate captions?

The current version focuses on script, voice and background video generation. Automatic burned-in captions are not currently included in this version.

Where is the website hosted?

The frontend can be hosted on Vercel while the Python/FastAPI video-generation backend runs separately on a service such as Render.

Can I cancel Creator Pro?

Creator Pro is a monthly subscription. Manage cancellation and subscription details through the payment provider used during checkout.
